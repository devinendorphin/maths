# Frozen experiments 51–55

This bounded batch implements the [literature-informed proposal](../research-notes/Roadmap-after-literature.md). Protocol and inputs are in `Protocol.json` and `Inputs.json`; source/kernel hashes are in `Freeze.json`. No gate or threshold is trained on these cases.

1. **51:** Exact Eisner–Severance-style endpoint/intersection recursion using a rational-parameter capacity-DP oracle. Compare the same oracle at every integer observation, original scalar maintenance and original indexed frontiers. All 27 cases have H64. Three repeats use six fresh non-normalized seed-1 cases. Native point solves are a separate fixture control. Oracle differences must be separated from reuse differences.
2. **52:** Controlled exact branch-and-bound cold/incumbent/terminal-partition reuse on 23 fresh or boundary inputs and 17 declared time points. All three modes share the same bound and branch rule. SCIP/PySCIPOpt is unavailable; this is a reference implementation, not a production-solver result. The external SCIP baseline remains outstanding.
3. **53:** Cached queue versus full horizon rescan under identical selective scalar repair, plus the original whole-partition maintenance comparison. Force two partial indexed builds to remain inactive before queue maintenance. Distinguish internal failures and packing changes, and include all decision/repair costs.
4. **54:** Compile the unchanged pinned official VIPR checker. Export native scalar proofs of original knapsack instances using explicit bounds, rounding and branch-assumption discharge. Check valid certificates and deliberately false final bounds. Pair same-packing endpoint proofs to certify affine intervals without changing feasible sets. The small interval connector is not formally verified.
5. **55:** Prove and exhaustively check the classical fixed-price rent-or-buy bound, then exhibit an unbounded variable-price failure of its unchanged threshold. Compare a declared bounded indexed construction probe with the earlier methods on the 27 cases. All probe and fallback costs are charged. No rent-or-buy theorem is transferred to CPU or renewable interval proofs.

Retrospective inputs are four earlier trajectories truncated to H64. The 18 fresh draws are held cases; five crafted fixtures are correctness controls, not random draws. Detailed evidence is separate from small source and summary files. Large historical experiment records are untouched.
