# Experiments 86–95: mathematical applicability and full reuse cost

Frozen before headline execution. Previous main: 649e3afb0ac730d7ed639cff97bdfe9fa8926ea9. These ten experiments use small, declared inputs to check conditional guarantees and expose their limits. They do not recreate the lost original 36–40 archive. Novelty assessment is a separate comparison with prior work, rather than a restriction on discovering a contribution.

| Experiment | Question | Declared comparison/control |
|---|---|---|
| 86 | Can two exact endpoint bounds support the same packing inside an interval? | Signed affine profits, rational interior checks, ties and crossings |
| 87 | What if the endpoint candidate has a gap? | Interpolated additive regret bound versus independently computed regret |
| 88 | When does an old bound survive a constraint change? | Capacity restriction with feasible candidate; expansion and infeasibility controls |
| 89 | Where does endpoint reasoning fail for nonlinear change? | Quadratic counterexample, curvature correction and sufficient margin test |
| 90 | Is certificate expiry the same as answer change? | Complete cell repricing versus every feasible competitor line; positive scaling controls |
| 91 | Does full proof reuse actually save measured work? | Cold points, envelope preparation and guarded envelope; three rotated repeats |
| 92 | How does observation frequency affect the same problem? | Matched dense, stride-eight and endpoint-only observations; full preparation charged |
| 93 | Which invalid receipts does the implemented connector reject? | Thirteen byte, metadata, scope and primal mutations per input; checksum is not authentication |
| 94 | What happens across model epochs? | Four cold solves versus two checked proofs and two exact-context cache hits |
| 95 | Does a known structural shortcut transfer to fresh inputs? | Dense 65-point streams, scaling/zero/random slopes and sign reversal; factor shortcut or guarded fallback |

All inputs and methods are predetermined in Inputs.json and Protocol.json. Six pre-freeze fixtures passed 46 method checks; random seeds are disjoint from the campaign. Algebraic counterexample templates are deliberately shared controls, not independent samples. No tuning or removal of difficult outcomes. Costs include required proof construction, writing, external checking and reuse validation; independent mathematical audit and archival work are outside algorithm timing. The existing bounded exact solvers retain their stated limits. This is not a production-solver comparison.
