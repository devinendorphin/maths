# Continue the larger inquiry from measured complete costs

The larger [changing-allocation inquiry](../projects/certified-allocation/Problem.md)
is underway. Exact flow/cut admission, sparse cost updates and bounded storage
were completed in 133–144; cost-only optimizer state in 145–147 and transactional
noncost primal repair in 148–151 supplied the measured next boundaries.

The specialist incremental comparison, 152–155, is now executed, independently
audited and replicated. It inspects actual residual state, adapts remaining
imbalance correctly, gates optimization on checked feasibility, retains state
through cuts and charges complete sessions. This closes the bounded comparison
left open in 148–151. It does not establish retained-price/tree maintenance or
a consistent end-to-end advantage over a cold specialist implementation.

The next small inquiry within the larger problem is exhaustive cost attribution.
On the largest cyclic network about 53% of warm query time lies outside the
current nonexhaustive phase timers. Freeze an accounting contract covering
transaction validation/copy/digest, ctypes marshalling, native patch and solve,
snapshots, feasible-witness admission, proof production/admission, proof ownership,
raw journal encoding/writing and final result production. Verify phase sums
against enclosing process/query clocks with explicit timer/observer overhead
and source-level boundaries. Keep the same mathematical queries and full
evidence so instrumentation cannot change the answer or quietly omit cost.

After that accounting question is closed, a bounded bridge/evidence experiment
may compare full scans with genuinely sparse native updates and compact state
receipts. Inspect capacity-clamp contributions, changed lower-bound offsets,
new balance deltas and stale hints after guard skips/cuts. Preserve exact rollback,
context/version binding and enough evidence to independently replay each native
state transition. Charge complete preflight/proof/record costs and retained
counter/state updates; do not label a native-kernel saving an end-to-end win.
The current full interface already amortizes startup over 49 queries, so longer
sessions alone cannot replace identifying the observed accounting remainder.

The larger goal remains affordable, inspectable certified allocations as
circumstances change. A successful compact/sparse pipeline would justify fresh
larger/dense/topology-changing families and a separately inspected solver-state
contract; it would not settle policy legitimacy. Retained dual/tree repair is
another distinct method and must demonstrate its own invariants rather than
being inferred from current primal residual reuse. Source priority review remains
open beyond these concrete implementation methods.

The application track still begins with a concrete consenting group, named
resources, eligibility, floors and priorities. No group or model is supplied
here, and synthetic supplies cannot stand in for people's needs or agreement.
No real allocation, human communication, deployment or merge is authorized by
the mathematical results. See the unchanged participation principles.

Externally authenticated updates/anchors and power-loss-safe persistence remain
separate application requirements. They do not reopen the already closed bounded
storage-format comparison without a concrete need.
