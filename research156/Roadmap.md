# Continue the larger inquiry with the measured Python costs

The [changing-allocation project](../projects/certified-allocation/Problem.md)
remains underway. The specialist state comparison in 152–155 and exhaustive
accounting in 156–159 are now executed, independently checked and replicated.
About two thirds of the largest observed warm query cost is transaction copying,
model validation and canonical encoding. Native patch/solve/snapshot/create
calls occupy only about 3.6%; timer and full recording costs are charged separately.
The next question therefore concerns representation and ownership before a
native-kernel or sparse-bridge optimization claim.

Freeze a bounded comparison of the current full-copy transaction against a
private, explicitly owned committed model and changed-edge/node copies. Keep
the public caller-isolation contract: mutation of submitted packets, returned
models, prior proofs, flow hints and records must not alter admitted state.
Reject malformed/stale/duplicate/infeasible-domain packets atomically, preserving
both the model and actual native state. Feasible versus shortage models remain
valid transactional commits under the existing mathematical contract.

Inspect validation/encoding provenance before deduplicating checks. A successful
private model admission must not become a forgeable shortcut around public
proof verification. Any cached digest or validation result must be bound to the
complete versioned model, context and actual immutable contents. Compare exact
integer-byte proof/model identities, counterexamples, guard/cut recovery and
all full native replay evidence against the existing engine. Develop on separate
small cases, then freeze fresh timing work before executing headline comparisons.

Retain matched observer-off full-process baselines and the interval ledger, but
report their footprint and run variability rather than subtracting an imaginary
timer correction. Keep the repaired checked recording path in every route. A
copy/check improvement must pay for its ownership registry, caches, proof work,
serialization and full evidence, not just an isolated update function.

After that experiment, genuinely sparse native patching and compact state
receipts may be justified by the remaining measured costs. Native marshalling
and some validation can still matter; the global validation category cannot
all be assigned to the bridge. Preserve capacity clipping, changed lower offsets,
remaining balances, stale hints after guard skips/cuts, exact rollback and enough
evidence for independent replay. Wider/dense/topology-changing families and
retained-price/tree methods require separate invariants and comparisons.

The application track still needs a concrete consenting group, named resources,
eligibility, floors and priorities. None is supplied by these synthetic models.
Participation principles, literature priority, authenticated update anchors and
power-loss-safe persistence remain separate open requirements. No human outreach,
real allocation, deployment or merge follows from a timing result.
