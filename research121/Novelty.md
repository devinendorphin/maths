# Originality assessment

This campaign continues the earlier mathematical question; it does not introduce a new arithmetic or claim the invention of objective sensitivity. The [earlier novelty audit](../research-notes/Novelty-audit-2026-10-08.md) and the recovered original-300 source/patch record remain part of the comparison.

The closest proof-producing DP precedent is Demirović et al., CP 2024, [*Pseudo-Boolean Reasoning About States and Transitions to Certify Dynamic Programming and Decision Diagram Algorithms*](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.CP.2024.9). Its official knapsack implementation is the basis of the recovered ≤16-item adaptation used here, with the documented signed-objective, item-bound and solution-expansion changes. We credit that algorithm and proof framework. This is an actual implementation comparison, not a Python recurrence checker presented as the published system. The new imported-parent checker uses unchanged checking rules and fork isolation; startup amortization is an operating-system technique, not a new inference rule.

VIPR is established by Cheung, Gleixner and Steffy, [*Verifying Integer Programming Results*, IPCO 2017](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-59250-3_13). The completion route follows Eifler and Gleixner, [*Safe and Verified Gomory Mixed Integer Cuts in a Rational MIP Framework* (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1137/23M156046X), the official [VIPR 1.1 specification](https://github.com/ambros-gleixner/VIPR/blob/30f2951d1e90e47afa821bdd1b12b82246656c42/cert_spec_v1_1.md), and the pinned SCIP exact-mode implementation. General independent certificate construction also has a close precedent in [Szeider, CP 2026](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.CP.2026.52); no general first-certificate claim is made.

Affine endpoint interpolation and positive scaling are elementary established mathematics. Parametric upper envelopes, caching, LRU eviction and optimization reoptimization are established. Kinetic certificate maintenance already separates certificate events from maintained-answer changes: Basch, Guibas and Hershberger, [*Data Structures for Mobile Data*, SODA 1997](https://www.ime.usp.br/~cris/aulas/19_1_6957/BaschGH-DSforMobileData.pdf). A simple empirical workload selector is not a novel learning method.

Implementation originality lies in the repository's concrete composition of compatible original-model binding, isolated checking deployments, interval admission and budgeted maintenance traces. Empirical originality lies in the newly frozen observations on seven named models, with complete route costs and explicit counterexamples/limits. Those contributions are assessable and reproducible; priority, a new theorem, general superiority and scalability have not been established. Differences of small runtimes are finite observations, not statistical significance claims.
