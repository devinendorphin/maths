# Originality assessment

The repaired work adds an audited implementation and finite experimental observations. It does not impose a rule against novelty claims. Mathematical, implementation and empirical originality require different evidence.

The affine endpoint implication follows by taking a convex combination of endpoint inequalities for a fixed feasible competitor. Positive objective scaling preserves order. These are established mathematics; the prior Lean proof checks their conditional use rather than claiming discovery. Kinetic certificate maintenance already distinguishes certificate events from maintained-answer changes: Basch, Guibas and Hershberger, *Data Structures for Mobile Data*, SODA 1997 ([primary paper](https://www.ime.usp.br/~cris/aulas/19_1_6957/BaschGH-DSforMobileData.pdf)). The earlier [novelty audit](../../research-notes/Novelty-audit-2026-10-08.md) gives the broader comparison.

Proof-producing knapsack DP has a close direct precedent: Demirović et al., CP 2024, [*Pseudo-Boolean Reasoning About States and Transitions to Certify Dynamic Programming and Decision Diagram Algorithms*](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.CP.2024.9). We did not recover its reported adaptation from the missing batch and do not describe our Python recurrence checker as that implementation.

The actual native certificate route uses established tools. Cheung, Gleixner and Steffy, [*Verifying Integer Programming Results*, IPCO 2017](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-59250-3_13), establishes VIPR. Eifler and Gleixner, [*Safe and Verified Gomory Mixed Integer Cuts in a Rational MIP Framework*, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1137/23M156046X), describes the incomplete-derivation framework. The official [VIPR specification](https://github.com/ambros-gleixner/VIPR/blob/30f2951d1e90e47afa821bdd1b12b82246656c42/cert_spec_v1_1.md) and source were consulted for completion. Official pinned [SCIP exact-mode source](https://github.com/scipopt/scip/blob/0c80fdd8e91d7d9f23c0c7a55b68884209d5f27c/src/scip/scip_exact.c#L176) establishes the reoptimization incompatibility; ordinary reoptimization is an existing feature.

Caching, immutable records, process isolation and avoiding repeated executable startup are established engineering. Our pristine-parent adapter, original-model normalization audit, compatible full-cost streams and explicit cache-epoch controls are original repository code. No exhaustive search establishes priority for that combination. In particular, the fork adapter provides deployment amortization, not a new proof rule.

The measurements are new evidence for these identified fixtures and implementations: admission savings on a stable factor fixture, costly rejected wide probes on a changing-optimum fixture, startup savings with isolated checking, and timing-order variation on a sparse SCIP comparison. They neither verify the missing report nor establish general performance/scalability. A potential implementation or empirical contribution remains assessable through a wider direct comparison; absence of a new theorem does not imply absence of contribution.
