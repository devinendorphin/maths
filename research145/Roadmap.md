# Continue the larger inquiry from measured boundaries

The original question remains: how can people keep allocations feasible,
explainable and provably optimal for explicit priorities as circumstances change,
without paying for a full solve at every step? See the unchanged
[problem charter](../projects/certified-allocation/Problem.md) and
[participation principles](../projects/certified-allocation/Participation.md).

| Bounded inquiry | Status / evidence | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Concrete exact flow/cut admission and service/coverage examples, 133–136 | Complete within its small frozen domain | Mathematical admission remains separate from human policy choice. |
| Sparse cost-delta handle and full charged fallback, 137–140 | Complete; largest cases saved time but full snapshots increased writes | Finish the storage question before expanding solver work. |
| Shared-base compact recovery, 141–144 | Complete; all cells reduced active writes/peak disk, all identities reproduced | Close this small inquiry under its flush/anchor contract. |
| Retained optimizer baseline and mixed-model boundary, 145–147 | Executed, audited and replicated; genuine cost-only tree reuse, explicit noncost resets and checked shortages | The larger changing-allocation inquiry has started; remaining work is below. |

The next bounded stage within that larger inquiry should target the observed
reset boundary: maintain a feasible flow after capacity/balance changes, then
recover optimality from retained optimizer state. Admit each repaired flow with
the same exact checker, or return a concrete shortage cut. Include reductions
below an old flow, balanced need increases, lower-bound changes and recovery
after infeasibility. Keep resets/failures in the comparison; a discarded failed
warm attempt still costs time. Freeze the update contract and guard limits before
running held-out streams.

Before any broader performance claim, use a specialist reoptimization solver
whose state/update semantics have been inspected, and add directed cyclic,
parallel-arc and non-transportation families with comparable complete sessions.
The current adapter is a credited warm baseline, not a substitute for that
comparison. Source priority review must inspect the complete methods rather
than infer novelty from a title, abstract or search hit.

The application track should begin from a concrete consenting group with named
resources, eligibility, service floors and priorities. Ask whether the objective
represents their decision, certify feasibility and shortages, and keep the model
small enough to inspect. A cost-minimum certificate does not settle fairness.
The synthetic streams in this repository cannot stand in for stakeholder input.

Recovery work remains scoped: externally trusted anchors, authenticated complete
deltas and power-loss-safe multi-file persistence need separate designs if the
application requires them. They do not reopen the completed comparison of the
two logical storage formats. No real allocation has been executed for anyone.

This roadmap is a continuation of the existing larger inquiry. Its open items
are not claimed complete by the present batch, and no deployment or merge is
authorized by these research results.
